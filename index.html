Edge ML Inference Benchmark

How fast a real-time object detector runs on Apple silicon, and what speed costs in accuracy. YOLOv8n at FP32, FP16 and INT8 on the CPU, GPU and Neural Engine of an M4 Pro and an iPhone: 16-bit on the Neural Engine ran 8× faster than 32-bit on the CPU, at equal accuracy. Diagnosed why naive INT8 detects nothing, then recovered most of INT8's accuracy with per-channel calibration and per-layer mixed precision. An iPhone app runs the detector live on the camera; on a still photo, its full image-to-boxes pipeline takes 1.6 ms on the Neural Engine.

BTC Survivor

Can tiny neural networks learn to predict a market live, well enough to pay real costs? Populations of online-learning networks paper trade against coin-flip twins that trade at the same moments but pick their side by coin flip, and every expected outcome is written down before launch. Version 1 trades Bitcoin around the clock. Version 2, scheduled for October 19, will trade seven large tech stocks and QQQ and test whether news scored by Claude predicts the next 30 minutes.

The experiments: v1 and v2

v1 · Bitcoin, live since Oct 2026. Six runs of 20 learners and 20 twins each:

  • A, main: does the 30-minute forecast correlate with the real move? Scored after 28 days of healthy data.
  • B, low fee: the same schedule at a 5 bps fee instead of 90.
  • C, machinery: starting at $500 so bots die, it checks that death, tournament replacement, mutation and lineage work live.
  • D and D2, 4-hour horizon: do bigger moves let predictions clear costs, and what does strict feed-stall handling cost?
  • E, frictionless: with fees, slippage and short charges set to zero, do the bots' own calls make money against their twins?

v2 · stocks and news, scheduled for Oct 19. S1 learns from prices only. S2 adds news from SEC filings, the Fed, BLS and market headlines, scored by Claude Haiku with FinBERT as fallback. Each S2 bot starts as an exact copy of its S1 counterpart, so any difference between the runs comes from news.

Playback Speed and Transcription Accuracy · co-first author, under review at the Journal of Emerging Investigators
